ABOUT

Poise Sports is the elite athletic division of Poise Entertainment Group. We serve as a premier career management liaison, bridging the gap between world-class talent and the global sports industry.

HOW DO WE WORK?

We combine deep industry insight with a holistic approach to recruitment. By moving beyond traditional scouting, we uncover and nurture raw potential, effectively changing the trajectory of player development. Our foundation of integrity and resilience drives us to find "diamonds in the rough" at every level of the game, providing the resources athletes need to reach their absolute peak.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

The core philosophy at Poise Sports is rooted in supporting talented individuals who possess the right mindset and a sincere desire to reach the top of their game. We take on the responsibility of managing your career’s business and personal logistics so that your primary focus can stay on the field. Leveraging our extensive industry connections and comprehensive support systems, we are fully committed to ensuring you reach your full potential and fulfill every ambition.
